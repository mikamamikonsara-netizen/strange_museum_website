03 WHY WE KEEP THEM
Some evidence asks not to be explained.
The museum preserves objects at the border between record and rumour—things found, catalogued and carefully left unresolved.
We document provenance, material change and witness testimony without forcing certainty where none exists. The archive is a place for disciplined attention.
Each object remains available to researchers, artists and the curious public. Doubt is not a flaw in the record; it is part of the record.
